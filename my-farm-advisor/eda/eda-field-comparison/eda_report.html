What the data says

  • Scale separates the regions before anything else does: 16 Harden paddocks have a median 23.6 ha, while six lease blocks have a median 351.7 ha and hold 63.4 % of the footprint.
  • The rotation is a two-crop system. Canola to wheat is 36.1 % of 122 transitions and wheat plus canola is 80.6 % of cropped field-years.
  • Season dominates zone: rainfall runs 688 mm in 2022 against 226 mm in 2023, while 2026 is 8.9 % warmer than 2022 on growing degree days.
  • Canopy tracks both crop and rain — pasture leads at 0.475 median NDVI and lupin trails at 0.226; integral versus season rain has Spearman ρ = 0.34.

Where the paddocks are

Limits

  • Weather is sampled at each farm centroid, not per paddock; Kanoona's 16 fields share one 0.05° cell.
  • Regions differ in field size, crop mix and management at the same time, so cross-region differences are associations, not causal effects.
  • NDVI is a monthly best-scene composite. A missing month is reported as a gap and never interpolated.
  • The 10 not-managed field-years are excluded from every crop statistic, even where NDVI exists — no crop record means no crop to compare.
  • No yield, input or gross-margin data in this build, so the analysis describes canopy and rotation rather than profitability.
Every number here is computed by src/build_analysis.py from the committed CSVs in examples/; the long-form analysis is ANALYSIS.md.
